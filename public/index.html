Three sprint types.

01
Investor Demo Sprint

Investor-ready prototype and screens designed to close your next round.

Fundraise
Who it's for
Pre-seed and seed founders heading into investor meetings. You have the deck — you need the product to match.
What's included
UX strategy and user flow mapping. High-fidelity UI screens. Interactive prototype. Investor-ready presentation assets. Delivered in 7 days.
Outcome
A prototype you can demo live — not describe from a slide.
02
Developer Handoff Sprint

Production-ready designs your team can build from immediately. Full specs included.

Engineering
Who it's for
Founders with engineers ready to build who need designs that remove ambiguity and let the team ship without back-and-forth.
What's included
End-to-end screen design. Component structure. Interaction states and edge cases. Figma dev mode annotations. Handoff documentation. Delivered in 7 days.
Outcome
Designs your team can open in Figma and build from today.
03
Validation Sprint

Test your core assumptions with real users before you spend on development.

Research
Who it's for
Founders who want to test before they build. Something real in front of users within a week.
What's included
Core flow design. Clickable prototype. Test scenarios and script. Usability session facilitation guidance. Insight summary. Delivered in 7 days.
Outcome
Validated direction before a line of code is written.
