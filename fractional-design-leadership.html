The gap

Most teams don't need another designer.
They need someone to lead the design.

You've hired talented designers. You've built a product. But the output isn't cohesive, decisions get stuck in committee, and nobody's connecting UX strategy to business outcomes. You don't have a design problem. You have a leadership gap.

Hiring a full-time VP or Head of Design takes months and costs north of six figures. Fractional design leadership gives you that seniority this week — without the overhead, the equity negotiations, or the risk of a bad hire.
